ATTRIBUTE WARS - CHANGELOG

1.0 RC1 (2026)
--------------
- Added the final author credits:
  ATTRIBUTE WARS
  CREATED BY
  JOSE MANUEL PLAZA
  2026
- Added the MIT license for the materials whose rights belong to the
  copyright holder.
- Added a clean, reproducible distribution package with SHA-256 checksums.
- Gameplay is unchanged from version 0.12.
- Final visual validation in Fuse remains pending before the accepted 1.0.0
  release.

0.12 (2026)
-----------
- Rebalanced the difficulty curve with an explicit 99-level period table.
- Slowed the level 1 hunter from 50 to 65 frames per step.
- Adjusted levels 2-6 to 50, 40, 52, 42 and 34 frames.
- Smoothed the cycle progression from level 7 onward.
- Kept the minimum enemy period at 20 frames.
- Preserved the 3 starting lives, extra life every 1,000 points, 5-life cap,
  65,530 score cap and the 12/16/20 level objectives.

0.11.2 (2026)
-------------
- Fixed the hunter getting stuck when the player stood on an active barrier
  or was surrounded by barriers.
- Added direct pursuit, short-range interception and territorial pressure.
- Allowed the hunter to backtrack when that was the optimal route.
- Kept one full path search per frame at most.

0.11.1 (2026)
-------------
- Improved the hunter's short-term prediction.
- Reduced repeated or stale interception decisions.
- Preserved the enemy speed and the existing game rules.

0.11 (2026)
-----------
- Added smarter enemy pursuit to the three scenarios.
- Fixed the duplicated GAME OVER header.
- Preserved the single GAME OVER screen flow.

0.10 (2026)
-----------
- Added the endless level progression beyond level 3.
- Added the five-digit score, the 65,530 cap and session high score.
- Added one extra life per 1,000 points, up to 5 lives.
- Added LEVEL COMPLETE, the level bonus and the 1UP notice.
- Added the stable GAME OVER flow with separate key presses.

0.9.1 (2026)
